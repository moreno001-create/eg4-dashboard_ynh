Connect your EG4 inverter

Sign in with your EG4 Monitor app account. The inverter's Wi-Fi dongle uploads its data there, and this server reads it about once a minute.

Your EG4 password is saved on this server so it can keep reading the inverter; only the app can read it. Unofficial; not affiliated with EG4.
